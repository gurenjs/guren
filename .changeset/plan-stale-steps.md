For a plan with a baseline, `guren plan:next` now skips the steps whose context went stale since approval (RFC 0030 §4): a step that owns a stale element, or whose own elements and behaviours name one, is listed as blocked with the element, what names it and what the reference checks say against the application now, and the steps after it in its task, or in a task waiting for it, are listed as waiting. It returns the first step that is neither. When every step left is blocked or waiting it returns no step, clears the mark and says a person decides, and still exits 0. Reading the application imports the routes file; a draft is never read, and an unstamped or unjudged element, or an application that cannot be read, blocks nothing. The Stop hook gives up on the marked step when something it names but does not own has gone stale, and records the stall on the mark for `plan:next` to report; what the marked step owns is its own work in progress and blocks nothing. `plan:verify` reports the baseline's freshness and the stale context of the steps it ran (`staleContext` in `--json`) without changing their outcome. `plan:status` now also names the elements that reference an unstamped or unjudged element.
